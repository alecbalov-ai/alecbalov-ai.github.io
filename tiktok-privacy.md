# Privacy Policy - Ecosistem Publicare

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Ecosistem Publicare is a private tool used only by its operator, Alex Balov, to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok accounts.

## Data the tool accesses
With the operator's permission (Login Kit and Content Posting API), the tool receives an access token for the operator's own TikTok account and uses it only to upload and schedule the operator's own videos.

## Data we store
Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. No personal data of other people is collected, sold, shared or transferred to third parties. No analytics or advertising trackers are used.

## Revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings > Security and permissions > Apps and services.
